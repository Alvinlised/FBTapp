Privacy policy.

Effective 28 October 2026 · FBT Trip Logbook for iOS, version 1.0

FBT Trip Logbook is maintained by Alvinlised. This policy describes the current app and this support website. It distinguishes trip records stored on your device from information handled by Apple, Google and the website host.

Trip and location information

When you enable automatic logging and grant location permission, the app uses GPS coordinates, accuracy, movement and timestamps to detect journeys and estimate distance and duration. It records start and end locations, dates, times, route labels, work/personal classifications, purpose and logging source. GPS points are used during an active journey; the saved trip register contains start and end coordinates rather than a full saved route trace.

Trip records and preferences are stored locally in the app’s web storage. This release has no developer-operated server for receiving trip history and does not implement a trip cloud-sync service. Device or system backups may be handled by Apple according to your device settings.

Location access and your choices

Location monitoring starts when automatic logging is enabled and operates while the app is active. Reliable background or locked-screen trip detection is not provided by this version. You can turn logging off in the app or revoke location access in iOS Settings. Revoking permission stops future access; it does not erase previously stored trips.

Advertising and Apple purchases

The iOS build includes Google Mobile Ads and an Apple StoreKit ad-removal purchase interface. When an ad request is made, Google may receive network and device information, including IP-derived approximate location, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic data, for ad delivery, measurement and related service operations. The app does not explicitly send its trip register or saved GPS coordinates as ad-request parameters. Test ads do not mean the SDK makes no network requests.

Read Google’s privacy policy and Google’s iOS SDK data disclosure for the provider’s practices. Data processing depends on the SDK and configuration in the installed build.

Apple handles payment and store-account information. The app checks StoreKit product and entitlement information and stores an ad-removal status locally. The developer does not receive your payment card details through the app. See Apple’s privacy policy.

Exporting, retention and deletion

Trip records remain on the device until app data is removed, subject to device storage and backup behaviour. There is no automatic expiry of trip records. The app currently has no dedicated delete-all control. Removing the app and its data through iOS, rather than offloading it, removes its local records; backups or exported files may remain separately.

CSV exports can contain sensitive journey details. Export only to destinations you choose and trust, and manage or delete exported copies separately. The developer cannot recover your device-only trip records. Export before removing the app.

Support enquiries

If you contact support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond and investigate your request. Avoid sending exact locations, full trip registers or financial information. Correspondence is retained as needed to handle the enquiry and related follow-up. You can ask us about access to or deletion of correspondence using the contact below.

This website and external services

This website has no sign-in, advertising scripts, analytics scripts, contact form or application cookies. The hosting provider may process connection information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate the website. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub’s privacy statement applies to hosting. Email links open your chosen email application. External links are subject to the destination’s policies.

Children

The app is intended for drivers keeping work and personal travel records and is not designed as a service for children. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information in a support enquiry.

Policy updates

Changes to app features or service providers may require an updated policy. The effective date above identifies this version. Material changes will be reflected here with the applicable app release.
