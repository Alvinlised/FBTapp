Start recording

  1. On Today, enable automatic logging.
  2. Allow location access when iOS asks.
  3. Keep the app active during the journey.
  4. Review any uncertain journeys and add a meaningful purpose for work trips.

Prepare an annual report

  1. Open Reports in the bottom navigation.
  2. Select the FBT year: 1 April to 31 March.
  3. Review any unclassified trips.
  4. Choose Export annual CSV. In a supported browser, Print / Save PDF opens the print dialog.

Common questions

Why did the app miss a trip?

Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services and the app’s location permission. Turn on the app’s automatic logging control and use a clear GPS signal. This version requires the app to remain active; background and locked-screen logging are not reliable. On Today, use Add a missed trip to enter the route, date, distance and purpose.

How do I change a trip’s classification?

Open Trips and use All, Work, Personal or Review to find it. Tap its classification label to change it or open the review prompt. Review all uncertain journeys before relying on an annual summary.

How is business use shown?

The current dashboard and annual report show work kilometres divided by all recorded kilometres for the selected FBT year. Unreviewed journeys remain in the total, so the percentage is provisional until review is complete. GPS estimates are not vehicle odometer readings.

Does this calculate my FBT bill?

No. Reports are summaries of recorded journeys. They do not calculate taxable value or FBT payable, establish a representative logbook, or replace vehicle cost, odometer and availability records. Confirm the appropriate treatment with your employer or registered tax adviser.

What if CSV export or printing does not work on my iPhone?

CSV export and the print action depend on the browser or native app environment. If no file or print dialog appears, contact support with the device and iOS version. Keep the app installed and retain your records while we investigate. Do not remove the app as a troubleshooting step before exporting data.

How do I restore an ad-removal purchase?

Open the avatar / Settings button, then Restore purchase. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Purchases are available only when the store product is configured and available for your build. If restoration fails, contact support with the message shown; do not send payment details.

Where are my trips stored?

Records are stored in the app’s on-device web storage. There is no developer-operated trip account or cloud sync in this release. Deleting the app or clearing its storage can remove records. Export a CSV before removing the app.
